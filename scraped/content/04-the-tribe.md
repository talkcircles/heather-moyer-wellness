## Find Your Tribe — Heal Together
Led by Heather Moyer, a certified grief educator and transformational coach based in Mt. Shasta, California, our weekly Grief & Healing Support Circle offers a safe space to connect, heal, and thrive.

Grief brings new challenges every day, but you don't have to face them alone.

## What is the Grief & Healing Support Circle?
Heather's Tribe is a virtual haven for anyone seeking to process grief, find hope, and connect with others on a similar journey.

Founded in 2020 as part of Heather Moyer Wellness, this inclusive community is inspired by Heather's own path through the loss of her son, Hunter, in 2022.

## What You'll Experience — Benefits of a Group
- **Consistent Support** — Weekly meet-ups to address new challenges, questions, and emotions as they arise.
- **Safe Space** — A compassionate environment to share tears, stories, and struggles without judgment.
- **Yoga for Grief** — Release pain and trauma through gentle, live yoga sessions designed for emotional healing.
- **Community Connection** — Build bonds with like-minded Wounded Warriors who understand your journey.
