# FAQ — heathermoyer.co

## Mt. Shasta flights & ground transportation?
The nearest airports are the Redding Municipal Airport (RDD) located in Redding, CA, 60 miles to the south, and the Rogue Valley International Medford Airport (MFR) located in Medford, OR, 86 miles to the north.

Additional options include Sacramento International Airport (214 miles away) and San Francisco International Airport (288 miles away). Ground transportation is not included in retreat costs, though Heather offers help arranging shared transportation.
